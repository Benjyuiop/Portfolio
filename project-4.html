GREENPULSE - AI-IOT URBAN FARMING

UI EXAMPLE AND HARDWARE

Project Overview

GreenPulse is an innovative IoT platform that combines real‑time sensor data with artificial intelligence to enable precision agriculture and sustainable urban farming. At its core, the system uses an ESP32 microcontroller connected to a suite of sensors that continuously monitor critical soil and environmental parameters: moisture levels at multiple depths, ambient temperature, humidity, and light intensity. This data is transmitted wirelessly to a cloud‑based Python backend hosted on Firebase, where it is stored and analyzed.

A custom AI model, trained on historical crop data and integrated with a weather API, predicts irrigation needs based on current conditions and short‑term forecasts. When the model determines that water is required, the system automatically activates a relay‑controlled irrigation network, delivering precise amounts of water to individual plant zones – reducing waste and preventing over‑ or under‑watering.

Users can monitor the entire operation through a responsive web dashboard that displays live sensor readings, historical trends, and AI‑driven recommendations. The dashboard also allows manual overrides and threshold adjustments. By automating irrigation decisions with intelligence rather than simple timers, GreenPulse conserves water, improves crop yields, and provides urban farmers with actionable insights, all while running on low‑cost, energy‑efficient hardware.
